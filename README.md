## BC WildWatch – Smart Campus Animal Safety & Incident Reporting System

BC WildWatch is an innovative mobile application developed to enhance safety and awareness across Belgium Campus ITversity by providing a centralized platform for reporting, monitoring, and managing animal-related incidents on campus.

As campuses continue to grow and interact with surrounding wildlife, students and staff may encounter animals that could pose safety concerns. BC WildWatch was created to bridge the communication gap between campus users and administrators by enabling quick reporting, efficient incident management, and timely responses.

The application empowers students to actively contribute to campus safety while providing staff members with the tools needed to investigate, manage, and resolve incidents effectively.

## The Inspiration Behind the Project

During daily campus activities, students occasionally encounter animals in parking areas, walkways, residence zones, or outdoor learning spaces. Traditionally, these sightings may go unreported or be communicated through informal channels, making it difficult for campus management to respond promptly.

BC WildWatch was designed to solve this problem by creating a digital reporting ecosystem where every sighting can be logged, tracked, investigated, and resolved through a structured process.

The goal was not only to improve campus safety but also to promote responsible interaction with wildlife and create a smarter, data-driven approach to incident management.

## Key Features
# Secure User Authentication

Students and staff can create accounts and securely access the platform using their campus credentials.

Features include:

- User registration
- Secure login
- Password recovery and reset
- Campus-specific account management
- Animal Sighting Reporting

Users can quickly report animal sightings directly from their mobile devices.

## Report information includes:

Animal type
Campus location
Incident category
Detailed description
Date and time of occurrence

This ensures that important information is captured accurately and stored for further investigation.

## 📊 Incident Monitoring & Tracking

Users can view the status of their reports and stay informed throughout the resolution process.

Capabilities include:

Viewing submitted reports
Monitoring active incidents
Tracking progress updates
Receiving feedback from administrators
🛡️ Staff Administration Portal

## Authorized staff members can manage and respond to reported incidents through a dedicated administration interface.

Administrative functions include:

Reviewing submitted reports
Assigning severity levels
Updating incident statuses
Recording response times
Adding resolution notes
Closing completed incidents

This creates accountability and ensures every report is addressed appropriately.

# Virtual Assistant Integration

To improve accessibility and user support, BC WildWatch includes a virtual assistant accessible through QR-code technology.

The assistant helps users:

Understand reporting procedures
Access frequently asked questions
Navigate the application
Receive immediate guidance when needed
